## Summary

Research-based guidelines for dementia care environments. Spatial features directly affect ADL performance, wandering, agitation, and social engagement.

## Principles

- Familiarity principle: home-like environments with familiar furniture improve ADL performance
- Reduce visible exits to decrease elopement attempts without physical restraint
- Circular (loop) walking paths reduce dead-end wandering and associated agitation
- Memory boxes/cues at bedroom doors improve room identification and reduce distress
- Dining room design: small tables (4-6 people), high-contrast tableware, simple layout improves nutrition intake
- High, even, glare-free illumination supports retained visual function; circadian lighting improves sleep
- Noise control: reverberation is especially disorienting for people with dementia
- Secure outdoor access significantly reduces agitation and physical aggression
- Personalization of rooms preserves identity and reduces depression
- Indirect staff supervision (acoustic and visual monitoring) preserves dignity while ensuring safety
