## Summary

Exterior cladding systems for wood light frame: WRBs, cladding types, and moisture management detailing.

## Principles

- Building enclosure priority order: control water, air, vapor, and heat transfer
- Water-resistive barrier (WRB / housewrap) is the last line of defense; cladding is the first-line rainscreen
- Rainscreen principle: drained and back-ventilated cavity between cladding and WRB dramatically improves durability
- Flashing at all penetrations and transitions — most water intrusion is flashing failure
- Continuous air barrier must be detailed at every assembly transition
- Cold climates: vapor retarder on warm side; hot-humid climates: vapor retarder on exterior side
- Fiber cement siding: durable, paintable, fire-resistant — most common medium-density residential cladding
- Wood siding: back-prime all pieces; ventilated cavity; 1in clearance from grade; decay-resistant species
- EIFS requires perfect installation and drainage — failures are expensive and common
- Thermal bridging through framing reduces effective wall R-value 20-40%; continuous exterior insulation eliminates bridges
