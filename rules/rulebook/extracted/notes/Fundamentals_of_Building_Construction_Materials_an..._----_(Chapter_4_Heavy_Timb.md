## Summary

Heavy timber framing, post-and-beam systems, and contemporary mass timber (CLT, NLT, DLT, GLT). Structural behavior, fire performance, connection design.

## Principles

- Heavy timber definition: minimum dimensions (6x8in posts, 6x10in beams) achieve fire resistance by char layer
- Mass timber is both structural and finish — exposed surfaces require higher material specification quality
- CLT: alternating grain layers provide two-way spanning capacity and dimensional stability
- Glulam: bending-efficient, available in very long spans; precamber for live load deflection
- NLT (nail-laminated) and DLT (dowel-laminated) are economical one-way slab systems
- Unprotected mass timber achieves 1-2hr fire ratings if minimum char dimensions maintained
- Acoustic performance of mass timber is inferior to concrete/steel — requires additional treatment
- Moisture management critical: mass timber must be dried before enclosure and protected from construction moisture
- Concealed connections (hidden steel plates, slotted connectors) preserve exposed aesthetic
- Mass timber stores biogenic carbon and substitutes for high-embodied-carbon steel and concrete
