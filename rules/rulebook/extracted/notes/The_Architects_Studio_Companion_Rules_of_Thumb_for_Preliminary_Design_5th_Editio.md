## Summary

Quick-reference guide for structural systems, building systems, and site design rules of thumb for schematic design decision-making.

## Principles

- Structure selection: wood to ~40ft spans, steel/concrete to 100ft+, long-span systems beyond
- Beam depth rules of thumb: steel W-shape = span/20; wood beam = span/15; reinforced concrete = span/12-15
- Column bay: 20-30ft bays are most economical for steel/concrete office construction
- Floor-to-floor height: structural + MEP + finish = typically 12-14ft commercial; 10-11ft if budget constrained
- Lateral system: shear walls to ~10 stories; moment frames to ~25 stories; braced frames to ~40 stories
- HVAC: allow 1 ton (12,000 BTU) per 200-400 sq ft depending on climate and use
- Plumbing stacks: group wet areas to minimize piping runs and structural penetrations
- Elevator count: 1 elevator per 45,000-50,000 sq ft gross area for commercial office
- Gross-to-net efficiency: office 75-85%; hospitals 55-65%; residential 80-90%
- Fire egress: travel distance to exit stair <=200ft in sprinklered office (IBC)
- Mechanical room: allow 3-8% of gross building area as starting estimate
