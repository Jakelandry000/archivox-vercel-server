## Summary

Evidence-based site design principles for aging-in-place and senior living environments.

## Principles

- Accessible paths: maximum 1:20 running slope, 1:50 cross-slope; rest nodes every 100-150ft
- Seating: bench heights 17-19in with armrests for rising assistance; shade/shelter every 100ft
- Lighting: minimum 10 footcandles at grade for night safety; avoid sharp glare transitions
- Outdoor seating must be visible and reachable from indoor common areas to encourage use
- Wayfinding: large-print signage, high-contrast color, tactile differentiation at decision nodes
- Thermal comfort: wind breaks, shade canopies, sun pockets for year-round outdoor use
- Multi-generational contact zones improve elderly mood and reduce social isolation
- Simple site geometry, consistent material language, landmarks at key decision points
- Fall prevention: slip-resistant even paving, no level changes, handrails on all ramps and stairs
- Excessive security measures reduce independence — design for monitored freedom, not lockdown
