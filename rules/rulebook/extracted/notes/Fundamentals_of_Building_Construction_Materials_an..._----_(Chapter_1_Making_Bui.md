## Summary

Introduces the building construction industry, project delivery methods, the architect's role, and the design-to-construction process.

## Principles

- Building is a team sport: architect, structural engineer, MEP, contractor, and owner have distinct roles
- Design-bid-build vs. design-build vs. CM-at-risk: delivery method affects design freedom, cost certainty, and schedule
- Working drawings must be constructable: dimensions should be verified against real material sizes
- Shop drawings and submittals must be reviewed, not rubber-stamped
- Building codes are minimum standards; design should exceed them where quality and safety warrant
- Change orders are expensive; resolve design ambiguities in design development, not in the field
- Long-lead items (structural steel, custom curtain wall) must be identified and ordered early
- Value engineering applied before CDs is legitimate; post-CD VE compromises design intent
- Punch list and substantial completion: architect's final verification that the building meets contract requirements
- Constructability review by structural engineer before contract documents should be standard practice
