## Summary

IPCC 2023 synthesis of climate science, impacts, and mitigation pathways. Scientific baseline for 1.5C/2C scenarios informing all sustainable design standards.

## Principles

- Current trajectory: 2.4-3.5C warming by 2100 without accelerated policy action
- 1.5C pathway requires net-zero CO2 by ~2050 globally — buildings sector must decarbonize completely
- Some climate impacts are unavoidable regardless of mitigation — design must account for locked-in change
- Extreme events (heat waves, floods, wildfires) are already more frequent and intense
- Urban heat island: cities 1-3C warmer than surrounding rural areas
- Ecosystem services provide regulating benefits that engineered solutions cannot always replace
- Climate impacts disproportionately affect the global south and low-income communities — address justice dimensions
- Buildings sector mitigation potential is large and cost-effective relative to other sectors
- Carbon budgets are finite: delay is costly — every year locks in more warming
- Align project emissions with sector decarbonization pathways, not just code compliance
