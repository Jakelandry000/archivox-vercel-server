## Summary

Comprehensive reference for passive design strategies organized by strategy type with rules of thumb and climate applicability.

## Principles

- Site microclimate analysis must precede any design decisions: solar access, prevailing winds, adjacent shading
- Heating-dominated climates: compact form, high insulation, south glazing with thermal mass
- Cooling-dominated climates: shading first, natural ventilation second, earth coupling third
- Overhang design: block noon sun on summer solstice, admit noon sun on winter solstice
- Cross-ventilation requires openings on opposite sides; stack ventilation requires vertical inlet/outlet separation
- Daylighting autonomy: position work surfaces within 1.5x ceiling height distance from window
- Thermal mass rule of thumb: 30-40 BTU/degF per sq ft of south glazing in heating climates
- Evaporative cooling effective below 30% relative humidity
- Ground-coupled cooling: earth temperature at 8ft depth approximates mean annual air temperature
- Photovoltaics: south-facing roof at tilt=latitude provides near-optimal year-round yield (northern hemisphere)
- Wind protection: dense tree rows perpendicular to prevailing wind reduce heating loads 15-25%
